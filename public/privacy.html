Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what information Suhail's Meridian Healing ("we") collects through this app, why, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, and the name, age and gender of each patient registered under it.
  • Photos you send for each healing session.
  • Your answers: the problems you face, treatments you have tried, medicines you take, and the changes you feel after each healing.
  • Payment details: the amount and whether it was paid. UPI payments are handled by your UPI app and bank – we never see your bank account or UPI PIN.
  • Your consent, and the date and time you gave it.
  • A small cookie that keeps you signed in on your phone for 12 hours, and your internet address, used only to protect the app against misuse.

Why we use it

  • To give you your healing sessions and follow your progress.
  • To confirm your payments.
  • To remind you about your sessions on your registered mobile number (for example on WhatsApp).

Who can see it

Only Suhail and authorised staff, through a password-protected dashboard. We do not sell your information, and we never share or publish your photos or answers. We use trusted service providers to run the app – the company that hosts our server, and WhatsApp for reminders – and they may only use your information to provide that service to us.

How we protect it

The app uses a secure (HTTPS) connection, photos and answers are stored on a private server, and the dashboard is protected by a password. Backups are kept so your records are not lost.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are receiving healing. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

For a patient under 18, a parent or guardian should send the photo and details and give consent.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Contact us using the details below.

Contact us

Please contact us through the clinic.
